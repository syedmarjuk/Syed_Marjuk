About

Six years between the microscope and the satellite

I am an Environmental Impact Assessment specialist and interdisciplinary marine biologist working at the point where field ecology meets orbital remote sensing and machine learning.

Over six years I have run ecological baseline surveys, marine biodiversity assessments and regulatory EIA reporting under Indian MoEF&CC norms — serving as EIA Biologist on the Ennore Oil Spill Impact Assessment (CPCL), the Karaikal Port Baseline Survey, and the TNEB Tuticorin & Tirunelveli Baseline Survey.

My doctoral work, funded by ISRO's Space Applications Centre, built AI-enhanced predictive models for plankton biomass in the southwestern Bay of Bengal — translating trained neural networks into band-math expressions that run pixel-wise across satellite imagery. I am a trained ornithologist (SACON, MoEF&CC), an expert taxonomist from Protozoa to Mammalia, and a certified PADI Open Water Diver.
