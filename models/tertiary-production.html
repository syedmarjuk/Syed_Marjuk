Model 03 · Four-tier trophic transfer

Tertiary production & food chain model

A satellite-driven trophic-transfer chain for the southwestern Bay of Bengal. It takes one satellite pixel — sea surface temperature, chlorophyll-a, light and water clarity — and carries it through four tiers to a potential fishery yield, calibrated against 1,022 fishery-independent survey sets.

The finding

The mesozooplankton pathway does not close

Built to estimate fishery production, the chain instead produced a diagnosis. That diagnosis is the result.

0.78% of primary production

The mesozooplankton route carries less than one percent of primary production to fish. The literature expects 10–20 %, so the pathway is short by roughly nineteen-fold.

Two mechanisms explain it

Microzooplankton, which a 200 µm net does not retain, and the benthic detrital pathway. Neither is captured by a net-based secondary production term.

Anchoring differently gives 10.7%

Anchor the chain on primary production rather than on measured mesozooplankton and transfer efficiency lands at 10.7 % — matching both Tamil Nadu demersal landings and the textbook value.

A case for regional algorithms

PACE chlorophyll shows no correlation with measured chlorophyll at 14 in-situ stations (ρ = +0.22, p = 0.14). A direct argument for regionally tuned algorithms and for OCM-3's 360 m resolution over a 4 km global product.

Benchmark

Machine learning against the mechanistic chain

Nested spatial block cross-validation, so a model cannot score well by memorising where the fishing happened. Tree ensembles win; the neural network and the stacked ensemble do not — a useful answer to a question reviewers always ask.

Bottom trawl · n = 531

Longline · n = 169

Modelled secondary production is the top SHAP predictor for the trawl fishery, which is the mechanistic chain earning its place inside a statistical model. The longline R² near zero is honest: pelagic longline catch is driven by processes this chain does not represent.

Inputs

What the model was built on

Fishery-independent survey

  • 726 bottom trawl sets — CRV Samudrika, 2016–18, kg h⁻¹
  • 296 longline sets — FORV Drushti, n per 100 hooks
  • Depths 26–129 m trawl, 20–4813 m longline
  • Mean trophic level 3.67 trawl, 4.34 longline, from FishBase

Satellite

  • 2,122 MODIS / CRW scenes, 2005–2025
  • 180 Oceansat-3 OCM scenes at 360 m LAC
  • Match-ups built at lags 0–4 days and compared
  • PACE assessed and parked — no temporal overlap with the CPUE

Open items, stated rather than hidden

  • CMFRI demersal landings for Tamil Nadu use a 31.5 % assumed share, and one pathway's calibration rests on it
  • Samudrika trawl specification is carried as a prior; real gear values would tighten the transfer-efficiency interval
  • The shelf mask and integration depth use water clarity as a depth proxy, pending GEBCO bathymetry
  • The three P/B growth models disagree roughly sixfold at 28 °C — propagated as a sensitivity, not chosen silently
