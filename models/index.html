Methodology

From cruise station to satellite pixel

1 · In-situ collection

WP-2 vertical hauls and oblique tows at oceanic stations across the Bay of Bengal and Arabian Sea. SST, salinity, dissolved oxygen, nutrients and chlorophyll-a recorded at every station with CTD profilers and Niskin bottles.

2 · Satellite pairing

Field measurements matched to MODIS-Aqua and Oceansat-3 OCM-3 ocean colour products in SeaDAS — building a paired dataset of satellite predictors against measured plankton biomass.

3 · Model training

MATLAB Regression Learner across ANN, SVM, Gaussian Process Regression and Ensemble Trees. Selection by R², RMSE and MAE against a held-out validation split.

4 · Pixel-wise deployment

The winning network is translated into a mathematical band-math expression and run pixel-by-pixel in SeaDAS — turning a point model into basin-scale biomass maps.
