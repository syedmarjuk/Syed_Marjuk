Architecture

How the networks are wired

fitnet 3-10-2 — regression

3Input
T · S · pH
10Hidden
tansig
2Output
EPR · NPR
  • Inputs normalised with MATLAB mapminmax to the range −1…1
  • Ten hyperbolic-tangent sigmoid hidden neurons
  • Linear output layer, then reverse-mapped to real units
  • Coefficient of determination R² = 0.9179

patternnet 3-10-3 — classification

3Input
T · S · pH
10Hidden
tansig
3Softmax
Low·Med·High
  • Shares the same input normalisation as the regression network
  • Softmax output gives a probability per production tier
  • Reported tier is the highest-probability class
  • Useful as a fast screen before committing culture resources

Method

From culture vessel to network

1 · Culture experiments

Systematic factorial trials on Acartia steueri across controlled temperature, salinity and pH combinations in the Marine Planktonology & Aquaculture Laboratory.

2 · Reproductive counts

Egg production rate and naupliar production rate enumerated per female per day under stereo zoom microscopy, with replicate cohorts per treatment.

3 · Network training

Levenberg–Marquardt training of a ten-neuron fitnet, selected on validation R²; a parallel patternnet trained on tiered production labels.

4 · Optimisation

The trained surface is searched for the parameter combination maximising output — the basis for the live optimiser on this page.
